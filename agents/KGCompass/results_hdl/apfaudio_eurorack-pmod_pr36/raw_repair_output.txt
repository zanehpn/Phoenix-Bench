The likely bug is in `gateware/cores/vco.sv`: the VCO advances its phase accumulator only on `sample_clk`, but unlike the generic `wavetable_osc`, it hardcodes the phase-to-table index bit start to `10 + FDIV`. After the clocking rewrite, the effective sample rate / phase increment scaling changed, and on ICE40 this likely causes the oscillator to run at the wrong rate or alias badly enough to appear broken.

The clean fix is to make `vco.sv` use the same phase indexing scheme as `wavetable_osc.sv`, and keep the `FDIV` offset only as a frequency divider on the phase readout, not as part of the lookup arithmetic in a way that can diverge across targets.
