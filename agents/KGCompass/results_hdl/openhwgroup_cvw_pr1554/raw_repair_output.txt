The bug is in the exception-priority logic in `src/privileged/trap.sv`.

### Localization
When Zicclsm is enabled, a misaligned load/store that is also an access fault must be classified as **misaligned** for loads/stores per the Privileged Spec priority rules. The current code checks access faults before the Zicclsm-enabled misalignment cases:

- `LoadAccessFaultM` is checked before `LoadMisalignedFaultM & P.ZICCLSM_SUPPORTED`
- `StoreAmoAccessFaultM` is checked before `StoreAmoMisalignedFaultM & P.ZICCLSM_SUPPORTED`

This causes the incorrect cause selection seen in the issue.

### Fix
Move the Zicclsm-enabled misaligned checks ahead of the access-fault checks.
