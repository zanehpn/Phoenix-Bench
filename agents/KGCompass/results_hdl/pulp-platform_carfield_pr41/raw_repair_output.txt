The bug is localized in `hw/carfield.sv` around the L2 memory integration.  
`carfield` currently routes AXI traffic to `l2_wrap`/`L2SPM` without inserting `axi_riscv_atomics_structs` in front of it, even though atomics support is required for all memories in `carfield`.

To fix this, add an atomics adapter stage before the L2 SPM-facing path, similar to how Cheshire does it for its main memory and other slave ports.
