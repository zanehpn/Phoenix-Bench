The bug is in `src/dmi_jtag_tap.sv`.

### Localization
`dtmcs.dmireset` is currently wired directly from the `dtmcs_q` register:
```systemverilog
assign dmi_reset_o = dtmcs_q.dmireset;
```
Since `dtmcs_q` is a shift/register field, the bit stays high after being written until another JTAG capture/update cycle overwrites it. Per spec, `dmireset` should be a write-one pulse, not a sticky level.

### Fix
Generate a one-cycle pulse when the DTMCS DR is updated with `dmireset` set, and clear the stored bit back to 0 afterward. This keeps the externally observed reset as a pulse while preserving normal capture/shift behavior.

  assign dmi_reset_o = dtmcs_q.dmireset;
=======
