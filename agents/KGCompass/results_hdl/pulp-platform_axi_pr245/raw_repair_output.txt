The bug is in `src/axi_lite_mux.sv` in the W-channel muxing logic.

### Localization
In the multi-slave-port case, `mst_w_chan` is currently driven by a conditional mux:

```systemverilog
assign mst_w_chan      = (!w_fifo_empty && !b_fifo_full) ? slv_reqs_i[w_select].w        :   '0;
assign mst_w_valid     = (!w_fifo_empty && !b_fifo_full) ? slv_reqs_i[w_select].w_valid  : 1'b0;
```

The issue suggests that the mux for `mst_w_chan` is redundant and that `mst_w_chan` should be tied to zero whenever `mst_w_valid == 0`. That implies the data path can be simplified to always select the indexed slave channel, while `mst_w_valid` still controls whether the beat is meaningful.

### Fix
Replace the conditional assignment to `mst_w_chan` with a direct assignment, while keeping `mst_w_valid` as-is.
