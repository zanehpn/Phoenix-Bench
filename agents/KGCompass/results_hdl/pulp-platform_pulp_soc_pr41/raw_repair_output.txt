The bug is localized in `rtl/pulp_soc/l2_ram_multi_bank.sv`.

### Why this is the problem
In the interleaved bank loop, the address presented to each SRAM bank is derived as:

```systemverilog
interleaved_addresses[i][INTL_MEM_ADDR_WIDTH+1:2]
```

This only strips the 2 byte-to-word conversion bits. For interleaved memory, it also must strip the bank-select bits used by the interleaving scheme (`$clog2(NB_BANKS)` bits). Because those bits are not removed, addresses alias once the addressed range exceeds what a single bank can uniquely represent.

---

### Fix
Use the interleaved address bits after removing both:
- the 2 LSBs for byte addressing
- the bank index bits for interleaving
