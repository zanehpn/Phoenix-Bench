The bug is localized in two places:

1. `hw/misc/clkrst_gen.sv`
   - `i2c_reset` is currently derived only from the 1 Hz/rst-delay logic, so it does not start low immediately and can be delayed by the `srst`-based sequencing.
   - `cam_en` and `i2c_reset` are only updated on the 1 Hz boundary, but the issue wants `i2c_reset` to be explicitly low until the delay counter reaches 4, and also wants `cam_en` available earlier to seed I2C setup.

2. `hw/lib/ip/i2c_master/i2c_top.sv` and `hw/lib/ip/i2c_master/i2c_ctrl.sv`
   - `i2c_ctrl` currently reloads `slave_address_plus_rw` only in `IDLE`, which means the first transaction can begin with an undefined/late value.
   - `i2c_top` does not provide any early-enable input such as `cam_en`, so the controller has no way to precompute the slave address before `i2c_reset` releases.
