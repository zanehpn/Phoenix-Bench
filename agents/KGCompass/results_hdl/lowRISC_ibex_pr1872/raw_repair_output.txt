In the provided RTL, `ibex_controller.sv` already has the relevant debug/trigger inputs and generates flush/debug control, while `ibex_id_stage.sv` receives `trigger_match_i`. The likely issue is that the controller does not treat a trigger-match-caused debug entry as a pipeline flush event for the coverage signal path.

The fix is to ensure the trigger-match debug event contributes to the controller’s flush/pipe-flush indication used by coverage.
